What this is

A 2.0-litre flat-plane V8 turning 16,000 rpm, built to power the VX-1. Flat-plane means all four crankpins lie in one plane, which gives an even firing interval on a 90-degree V and lets the engine rev; the penalty is secondary imbalance, which a light reciprocating assembly keeps tolerable. Run it and the crank, rods and pistons move on a real slider-crank solved live — the piston travel is the stroke to three figures because it is the same kinematics the geometry was built from.
